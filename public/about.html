About us

Agricultural inputs, made for the farmer.

SL Industries is an agri-inputs manufacturer based in Gorantla, Guntur, Andhra Pradesh. We produce a broad range of crop-protection and plant-nutrition products — insecticides, bio-pesticides, micronutrients, bio-stimulants and growth regulators — to help farmers protect their crops and improve their yields.

Our focus

Everything the crop needs, from one maker.

Our range spans the full crop cycle: insecticides and botanical protectors to manage pests, chelated micronutrients to correct deficiencies, and amino-acid, humic and protein-based bio-stimulants to support germination, flowering, fruit set and overall yield.

Products are manufactured under our own licences and packaged for use across major crops including paddy, cotton, chillies, pulses, vegetables, fruits and horticultural crops. Always read the product label and enclosed leaflet before use.
